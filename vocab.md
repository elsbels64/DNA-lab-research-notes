Linkage disequilibrium - how frequently two variants are separated by recombination
 - linkage disequilibrium can be high even if the recombination rate doesn't change (if you have a bottleneck where everyone with A and B dies then the disequilibrium will be high even if the recombination rate is 50%)

effective population size - n_e --- number of individuals in an idealized population that would experience the same rate of genetic drift.
